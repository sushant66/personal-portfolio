# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: engineering leaders, hiring managers, and technical recruiters evaluating
Sushant for senior/staff backend roles. They arrive skeptical, skim fast, and are
looking for evidence — scale, ownership, quantified impact — not adjectives. They
mostly land on a laptop during a workday, often mid-shortlist with many tabs open.

Secondary: founders and potential collaborators gauging range and initiative
(shipped side projects, AI tooling, 0→1 building).

The job to be done: in under 30 seconds, decide "this person ships production systems
at scale — worth a conversation."

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes the design as substantial as the résumé behind it.
Sushant has a genuinely strong record — production systems at BrowserStack/EDRA,
Jio/Fynd, and LTIMindtree, with hard numbers (90% faster loads, 100k+ orders/day,
20% fraud reduction, an IEEE publication) — that the current generic template buries.
Success is a site that reads as engineered by the person it describes: precise,
confident, evidence-first, and impossible to mistake for an AI template.

## Brand Personality

Precise · engineered · quietly confident.

Voice: an experienced engineer stating facts. Specific over superlative — "reduced
page load time by 90%," never "passionate about performance." Lets numbers and shipped
work carry the weight. Calm authority, no hype, no exclamation marks.

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel they are looking at something *built*, not
decorated — the way a well-architected system inspires trust before you read a line of it.

## Anti-references

- The current site: indigo (#6366f1) + Tailwind-slate, animated rainbow gradient text,
  identical card grids, glow-blob hero, scroll-mouse, uniform fade-up-on-scroll. The
  saturated AI-portfolio template.
- The "backend dev → green-on-black terminal / Matrix" cliché. Technical ≠ costume.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif italic, drop caps, broadsheet grid).
  Structural is not the same as editorial; stay schematic, not literary.
- Hero-metric template (giant number + tiny label + gradient accent). Present impact
  as a factual ledger, not a SaaS stat block.
- Numbered/uppercase-tracked eyebrows above every section as decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The page is itself a well-built system — considered
   structure, real hierarchy, nothing arbitrary. The craft IS the argument.
2. **Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim earns its place with a number, a company,
   or a shipped artifact. Cut anything a stronger fact could replace.
3. **Structure you can see.** A visible grid, hairline rules, and honest alignment do
   the decorative work. No ornament that isn't also information.
4. **One signal, used with discipline.** A single decisive accent marks what matters;
   restraint everywhere else makes the signal mean something.
5. **Fast is a feature.** For a systems engineer, performance is part of the brand.
   Near-zero JS, instant loads, perfect Lighthouse — the medium proves the message.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 (verified, not eyeballed — the
accent orange is for marks/large type, never small body copy). Full keyboard operability
with visible focus rings. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with real reduced-motion
alternatives for every animation. Respect `prefers-color-scheme`; provide a manual
light/dark toggle. Semantic landmarks and honest heading order.
